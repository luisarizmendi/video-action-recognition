Drop one or more .mp4/.mkv/.avi files here. camera-capture loops them
automatically when SOURCE_MODE=usb and no USB camera is found, or you can
force it with SOURCE_MODE=file if you add that mode. On a laptop with no
camera and no RTSP source, this is the easiest way to see the full pipeline
running: any video with people walking works for the Tier 1 gate and the
passenger counter, and a clip with two people moving quickly will trigger a
Suspicious or High safety event with the stub classifier.
